## Design Context

### Users

Exarchos maintainers reviewing technical evidence and making implementation,
sequencing, and issue-triage decisions.

### Brand Personality

Forensic, disciplined, and decisive. The interface should reward careful
inspection without feeling bureaucratic or visually generic.

### Aesthetic Direction

Reuse the established Exarchos dark technical-report language from
`.lavish/phase-gate-plan-walkthrough.html`: dense editorial composition,
restrained status color, strong typographic hierarchy, and scan-friendly
evidence surfaces.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision and the evidence that changes it.
2. Make live defects, partial fixes, and proof gaps visually distinct.
3. Prefer aligned comparisons and semantic tables over repeated cards.
4. Keep implementation sequencing and acceptance criteria directly actionable.
5. Use interaction only for explicit review choices and queued feedback.
