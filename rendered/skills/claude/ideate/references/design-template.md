# Design & Rationale

Use the [unified spec template](@skills/plan/references/spec-template.md) for the complete design section. Give each requirement a `DR-N` heading and observable acceptance criteria. Describe what happens on failure and at boundaries. Use Given/When/Then when it makes behavior easier to verify.

## Deep exploration

Document two or three materially different options. Explain how each addresses the problem and where each fails. Compare integration cost, operating constraints, and risk. Record why the selected option fits and what evidence informed the choice.

Under `### Exploration`, cite the local research note by path and by the same `correlationId` recorded in the note. An author can assign a stable identifier when creating the note.
